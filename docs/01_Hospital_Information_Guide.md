## 1. Purpose & Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
The purpose of this guide is to provide a complete, authoritative, and patient-centered orientation to SmartCare Multispeciality Hospital, its clinical departments, specialized medical services, operational hours, physical facilities, and navigation procedures. This document serves as the foundational reference manual for patients and the primary knowledge base for the SmartCare AI Assistant.

### 1.2 Scope
This guide applies to all outpatient departments (OPD), inpatient wards, diagnostic suites, day-care centers, and support facilities located at the SmartCare Multispeciality Hospital main campus in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

## 2. Hospital Overview & Philosophy

### 2.1 About SmartCare Hospital
SmartCare Multispeciality Hospital is a premier 450-bed quaternary care medical institution established in Hyderabad, Telangana. Designed with patient safety, clinical excellence, and ethical healthcare as core tenets, SmartCare provides comprehensive healthcare spanning 21 clinical departments, advanced intensive care units, modern modular operating theatres, an automated diagnostic laboratory, and comprehensive rehabilitative support.

- **Campus Address:** Road No. 12, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, Telangana 500034, India
- **Landmark:** Opposite KBR Park Green Enclave, Near Banjara Circle
- **Institutional Accreditation:** National Board for Hospital Accreditation (NABH) & National Accreditation Board for Testing and Laboratories (NABL) certified
- **Facility Type:** Multispeciality Tertiary & Quaternary Referral Center

### 2.2 Mission, Vision & Core Values
- **Mission:** To deliver compassionate, evidence-based, technologically advanced, and affordable healthcare to every patient with clinical rigor and human empathy.
- **Vision:** To be the most trusted healthcare institution in South India, renowned for patient safety, clinical breakthroughs, and seamless patient experience.
- **Core Values (C.A.R.E.):**
  - **Compassion:** Treating patients and families with dignity, empathy, and warmth.
  - **Accountability:** Transparent clinical communication, ethical billing, and medical integrity.
  - **Rigor:** Adherence to global clinical protocols and zero-tolerance for medical lapses.
  - **Excellence:** Continuous learning, state-of-the-art medical technology, and rapid response.

## 4. Clinical Departments & Specialty Directory

SmartCare Multispeciality Hospital houses 21 clinical and diagnostic departments. Every department is staffed with senior consultants, resident medical officers, and specialized clinical nurse coordinators.

### 4.1 General Medicine & Internal Health
- **Overview:** Primary diagnostic evaluation, chronic lifestyle disease management, multisystem infections, metabolic disorders, and geriatric health.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Hypertension, type 1 & 2 diabetes mellitus, infectious fevers (dengue, typhoid, malaria), thyroid dysfunction, unexplained fatigue, and adult immunizations.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
- **Location:** Level 1, OPD Block Wing A.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Complete blood count, HbA1c, lipid profile, liver & kidney panels.

### 4.2 Cardiology & Cardiothoracic Sciences
- **Overview:** Comprehensive cardiovascular diagnostic, interventional catheterization, and coronary surgical care.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Coronary artery disease, angina, myocardial infarction, heart failure, arrhythmias, valvular disorders, congenital heart defects.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
- **Location:** Level 2, Heart & Vascular Institute.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** 12-lead ECG, 2D/3D Transthoracic Echocardiogram, Treadmill Stress Test (TMT), 24-hr Holter monitoring, coronary angiography.

### 4.3 Neurology & Neuro-Surgery
- **Overview:** Diagnosis and treatment of central, peripheral, and autonomic nervous system disorders.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Acute ischemic/hemorrhagic stroke, epilepsy, migraine, Parkinson's disease, neuropathy, multiple sclerosis, brain and spine trauma.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
- **Location:** Level 3, Neurosciences Block.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** 3-Tesla Brain MRI, 128-slice CT Neuro, Electroencephalogram (EEG), Nerve Conduction Velocity (NCV), Electromyography (EMG).

### 4.4 Orthopedics & Joint Replacement
- **Overview:** Musculoskeletal trauma, joint reconstruction, sports medicine, and spinal disorders.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Osteoarthritis, rheumatoid knee/hip degeneration, ligament tears (ACL/PCL), fractures, disk herniation, osteoporosis.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM – 7:30 PM.
- **Location:** Level 1, Orthopedic Suite Wing B.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Digital musculoskeletal X-rays, Bone Mineral Density (DEXA) scans, high-resolution CT musculoskeletal.

### 4.5 Pediatrics & Neonatology
- **Overview:** Holistic infant, child, and adolescent healthcare, including Level-III NICU and pediatric intensive care.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Childhood infections, asthma, growth and developmental delays, neonatal jaundice, pediatric allergies, routine vaccinations.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
- **Location:** Level 2, Women & Child Pavilion.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Pediatric ultrasound, rapid pediatric viral panels, growth parameter telemetry.

### 4.6 Gynecology & Obstetrics
- **Overview:** Women's reproductive wellness, antenatal and perinatal care, high-risk obstetrics, and minimally invasive laparoscopic surgery.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Antenatal care, polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS), uterine fibroids, endometriosis, menopausal health, infertility.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
- **Location:** Level 2, Women & Child Pavilion Wing C.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** 4D fetal anomaly ultrasound, non-stress testing (NST), Pap smear, transvaginal sonography (TVS).

### 4.7 Dermatology & Cosmetology
- **Overview:** Medical, surgical, and cosmetic care for skin, hair, and nail pathology.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Psoriasis, eczema, severe acne, fungal infections, alopecia, vitiligo, skin allergy patch testing.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
- **Location:** Level 3, Specialty OPD Wing D.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Skin biopsy, dermoscopy, allergy patch tests.

### 4.8 ENT (Otorhinolaryngology)
- **Overview:** Diagnosis and microsurgery for ear, nose, throat, head, and neck conditions.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Chronic sinusitis, deviated nasal septum, tonsillitis, hearing impairment, tinnitus, vertigo, vocal cord polyps.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM – 7:00 PM.
- **Location:** Level 1, Ambulatory Care Wing.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Pure tone audiometry (PTA), tympanometry, diagnostic nasal endoscopy, videolaryngoscopy.

### 4.9 Ophthalmology
- **Overview:** Medical and microsurgical eye care, cataract extraction, retinal management, and refractive correction.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Cataracts, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, dry eye syndrome, corneal ulcers, refractive visual errors.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM – 6:30 PM.
- **Location:** Level 1, Vision Care Center.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT), slit-lamp examination, visual field perimeter testing, non-contact tonometry.

### 4.10 General & Minimally Invasive Surgery
- **Overview:** Elective and emergency surgical procedures using laparoscopic, robotic, and open techniques.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Hernia, gallstones, appendicitis, hemorrhoids/fistula, thyroid swellings, soft tissue tumors.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM.
- **Location:** Level 3, Surgical Sciences Pavilion.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Ultrasound abdomen, pre-anesthetic clearance panels.

### 4.11 Gastroenterology & Hepatology
- **Overview:** Management of luminal gastrointestinal diseases, liver pathology, biliary disorders, and therapeutic endoscopy.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Acid peptic disease, GERD, fatty liver, hepatitis, pancreatitis, ulcerative colitis, Crohn's disease, jaundice.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM.
- **Location:** Level 3, Digestive Health Center.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Upper GI Endoscopy, Colonoscopy, FibroScan, Liver function panels.

### 4.12 Pulmonology & Respiratory Medicine
- **Overview:** Chronic obstructive lung diseases, asthma, pulmonary infections, interstitial lung disease, and sleep disorders.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Bronchial asthma, COPD, pneumonia, tuberculosis, pulmonary fibrosis, obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM – 6:30 PM.
- **Location:** Level 2, Respiratory Care Suite.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Spirometry / Pulmonary Function Testing (PFT), chest high-resolution CT (HRCT), sleep study (Polysomnography).

### 4.13 Nephrology & Dialysis Services
- **Overview:** Kidney disease management, glomerulonephritis, hemodialysis, and peritoneal dialysis.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Chronic kidney disease (CKD), acute kidney injury, proteinuria, electrolyte imbalances.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Hemodialysis operates 24/7 in 3 shifts).
- **Location:** Level 4, Renal Sciences Wing.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Serum creatinine, eGFR, 24-hr urinary protein, renal doppler ultrasound.

### 4.14 Urology & Andrology
- **Overview:** Surgical treatment of male and female urinary tracts and male reproductive health.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Kidney stones (nephrolithiasis), benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), urinary tract infections, urinary incontinence, erectile dysfunction.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM.
- **Location:** Level 4, Uro-Renal Pavilion.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** Uroflowmetry, KUB ultrasound, CT urography, prostate-specific antigen (PSA).

### 4.15 Medical & Surgical Oncology
- **Overview:** Cancer diagnostics, multidisciplinary tumor board evaluations, chemotherapy day-care, and surgical resection.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Breast cancer, gastrointestinal cancers, lung cancer, hematologic malignancies, head and neck tumors.
- **OPD Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM.
- **Location:** Level 5, Comprehensive Cancer Center.
- **Diagnostics Associated:** PET-CT scan, tissue biopsy, immunohistochemistry, tumor markers.

### 4.16 Psychiatry & Behavioral Health

- **Common Conditions Treated:** Generalized anxiety, major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder, OCD, panic attacks, sleep disturbances.

### 4.17 Dentistry & Maxillofacial Surgery
- **Overview:** Preventive, restorative, orthodontic, and maxillofacial surgical dental care.
- **Common Conditions Treated:** Dental caries, root canal therapy, impacted wisdom teeth, periodontal disease, malocclusion.

## 5. Hospital Facilities & Amenities

SmartCare Hospital is designed to provide maximum safety, convenience, and comfort for patients and their accompanying caregivers:

1. **Central Reception & Information Concourse:**
   Located in the main ground-floor atrium. Provides bilingual registration, appointment check-ins, direction maps, wheelchair dispatch, and hospital guide booklets.
2. **Waiting Lounges & Seating:**
   Spacious, air-conditioned waiting lounges with digital token display screens, ergonomic seating, purified drinking water stations, and mobile charging docks located on every floor.
3. **Emergency Resuscitation & Observation Bays:**
   A 25-bed emergency center with life-support telemetry, ventilator backup, rapid blood gas analyzers, and isolation beds.
4. **Intensive Care Units (ICUs):**
   Specialized 60-bed critical care ecosystem comprising Medical ICU (MICU), Coronary Care Unit (CCU), Surgical ICU (SICU), Pediatric ICU (PICU), and Neonatal ICU (NICU) with 1:1 nurse-to-patient staffing ratios.
5. **Modular Operating Theatres:**
   10 sterile laminar-airflow operating suites with HEPA filtration, robotic surgical consoles, and integrated intraoperative imaging.
6. **Patient Support & Helpdesk Desk:**
   Dedicated concierge support on Ground Floor for elderly patients, non-local languages, senior citizens, and physically challenged visitors.
7. **Hospital Cafeteria & Dining:**
   Ground floor courtyard serving hygienic vegetarian and non-vegetarian balanced meals, fresh fruit juices, and diet-specific therapeutic snacks. Open 6:30 AM to 10:30 PM.
8. **Parking & Valet Services:**
   Three basement parking levels accommodating over 400 cars and 300 two-wheelers. Free 15-minute emergency drop-off zone at Gate 1 ramp. Complimentary valet parking for senior citizens and disabled drivers.
9. **Accessibility & Barrier-Free Infrastructure:**
   Wheelchair ramps at all exterior entrances, tactile pathways, extra-wide elevators with braille indicators and vocal floor announcements, handicap-accessible restrooms on every level.
10. **Prayer & Reflection Room:**
    Quiet multi-faith prayer and meditation sanctuary located on Level 4 Quiet Zone.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: What are the general OPD hours at SmartCare Hospital?**  
A: Outpatient clinics (OPD) operate from Monday through Saturday between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM. Individual doctor clinic schedules within these hours may vary.

**Q2: Is SmartCare Hospital open on Sundays?**  
A: The hospital emergency department, trauma center, intensive care units, inpatient wards, and pharmacy operate 24/7 on Sundays. Regular outpatient doctor consultations (OPD) are closed on Sundays.

**Q3: Where is the Emergency Department located?**  
A: The Emergency Department is located on the Ground Floor with a dedicated separate vehicle ramp at Gate 1, Banjara Hills campus.

**Q4: Is the pharmacy open 24 hours?**  
A: Yes, the SmartCare Main Pharmacy is located in the Central Atrium on the Ground Floor and operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including all public holidays.

**Q5: What time does the diagnostic laboratory open for fasting blood sample collection?**  
A: The main outpatient diagnostic laboratory opens at 6:00 AM Monday through Saturday for fasting sample collections and closes at 8:00 PM. Emergency STAT laboratory tests are processed 24/7.

**Q6: What is a UHID and do I need one?**  
A: UHID stands for Unique Hospital Identification number. Every patient is assigned a permanent UHID during their first visit. It securely indexes all your medical records, test results, and prescriptions across all departments.

**Q7: Can I visit the hospital without a prior appointment?**  
A: Yes, walk-in patients are accepted at OPD registration counters between 8:00 AM and 7:30 PM. However, pre-booking is strongly recommended to minimize waiting times. Emergency patients are treated immediately 24/7 without appointments.

**Q8: Where can I get an MRI or CT scan done?**  
A: The Department of Radiology and Diagnostic Imaging is located on Basement Level 1. Routine scans operate from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, while emergency trauma imaging is operational 24/7.

**Q9: Where do I park my car and is valet service available?**  
A: SmartCare has three underground basement parking levels (Basement 1, 2, and 3). Complimentary valet parking is available at the main porch for senior citizens, expectant mothers, and disabled visitors.

**Q10: What are the visiting hours for relatives of admitted patients?**  
A: For general and private inpatient rooms, visiting hours are 4:30 PM to 7:00 PM daily, and an additional morning hour on Sundays from 10:30 AM to 11:30 AM. For ICUs, visiting is strictly restricted to 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM and 5:00 PM – 5:30 PM for one attendant at a time.

**Q11: Does SmartCare provide ambulance service?**  
A: Yes. SmartCare operates Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) and Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulances 24/7 across Hyderabad. Call the Emergency Hotline at +91-40-6999-0000.

**Q12: How can I collect my lab test reports?**  
A: Routine test reports are automatically uploaded to the SmartCare Patient Portal and WhatsApp/SMS notification links. Physical stamped copies can be collected at the Laboratory Report Counter on the Ground Floor.

**Q13: Which department treats stomach pain, acidity, and liver issues?**  
A: The Department of Gastroenterology and Hepatology, located on Level 3 (Digestive Health Center), specializes in gastrointestinal and liver conditions.

**Q14: Does SmartCare have a specialized children's clinic?**  
A: Yes. The Department of Pediatrics and Neonatology is located on Level 2 in the Women & Child Pavilion, operating Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

**Q15: What payment modes are accepted at the hospital billing counters?**  
A: SmartCare accepts cash, debit cards, credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, and approved cashless health insurance claims.

**Q16: Where can visitors eat inside the hospital campus?**  
A: The SmartCare Cafeteria is situated on the Ground Floor adjacent to the Garden Courtyard, serving clean, healthy meals, snacks, and beverages between 6:30 AM and 10:30 PM.

**Q17: Is wheelchair assistance available at the entrance?**  
A: Yes. Dedicated wheelchair assistance and patient care attendants are stationed at the Main Entrance (Gate 2) and Emergency Entrance (Gate 1) 24 hours a day free of charge.

**Q18: What should I do if I lose an item inside the hospital?**  
A: Please contact the Central Security and Lost & Found Desk located at Ground Floor Wing A near the main security station, or call Extension 1044.

**Q19: Are interpreter or language translation services available?**  
A: Yes, our Patient Support Desk provides translation and assistance in English, Telugu, Hindi, Urdu, Kannada, and Marathi.

**Q20: Can I get my routine vaccinations at SmartCare?**  
A: Yes. Adult and pediatric vaccination services are provided through the Department of General Medicine and Department of Pediatrics respectively, Monday through Saturday.
